Before you book

Original work only

In the creative process, it is very important that you trust my work 100%: you bring me the idea and the concept of the tattoo, and the creation and the aesthetics are up to me.

I don't do copies (especially of my own work), reworks, touch-ups on other tattoos or cover-ups, unless agreed beforehand.

Pricing

Quote and deposit

Fixed prices, based on size, complexity and number of sessions.

Full sleeves

When a large project, such as a full arm sleeve, is planned all at once, things tend to fit together better and the whole becomes more harmonious. That is why there is a special discount for sleeves developed all at once, and larger projects end up being relatively more affordable.

If the size or body placement changes on the agreed day, the price is adjusted. So please be realistic about the size.
